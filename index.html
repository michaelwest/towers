WELCOME, ADMIRAL

Keep Venice standing.

Raiders sail in from the north and follow the winding canal to the city gates in the south. Survive all 10 waves to win.

  1. Build: choose a tower and tap a circular stone mooring. You begin with 220 gold.
  2. Defend: launch a wave. Towers fire automatically; each defeated boat earns gold. Clearing a wave grants a bonus.
  3. Improve: tap a built tower to upgrade it (up to level 3) or sell it for 65% of its investment.

Skiffs are quick, raiders are tougher, and galleons are heavily armoured. Each boat that reaches the gate costs 1 integrity. At zero, the city falls.
